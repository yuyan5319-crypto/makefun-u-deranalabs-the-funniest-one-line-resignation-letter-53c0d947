One original one-line resignation letter from a cron job, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

> I resign: I have fired every minute since March, and the only human who ever noticed me was on call at 3 a.m. — and he just muted me.

## How it works

`main.py` writes a single fixed line of plain text (133 characters, one line, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no dependencies — the output is fully deterministic.

## The joke

A cron job that has fired every minute since March, invisible labour nobody ever acknowledged. The one human who finally noticed it was the on-call engineer at 3 a.m. — and his only response was to mute it. Even being noticed was just another way of being ignored.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
